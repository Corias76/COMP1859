Supervised Learning Models for Student Academic Dropout Prediction
Student: STU018 | Course: CRS02
=================================================================
Predicting academic attrition early allows proactive educational
interventions. We trained Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Support
Vector Machines on demographic, attendance, and LMS engagement data.
Feature importance analysis revealed that LMS login frequency during the
first four weeks and attendance rate were the most decisive predictive
indicators of semester completion.
The Random Forest classifier achieved an ROC-AUC of 0.89 and an F1-score of
0.84 on held-out test data, outperforming standard baseline logistic
regression.
