Campus Navigation Optimization using Dijkstra and A-Star Graph Algorithms
Student: STU058 | Course: CRS05
=================================================================
Navigating multi-building university campuses efficiently between
consecutive lectures is modeled as a weighted shortest path problem. We
constructed a topological graph of campus pathways and elevators.
A-Star search with Euclidean distance heuristics reduced explored state
vertices by 64 percent compared to standard Dijkstra algorithm during peak
transition times.
We incorporated wheelchair accessibility constraints by dynamically
weighting elevation gain and stair obstacles, generating accessible route
recommendations.
