Deep Convolutional Neural Networks for Educational Diagram Recognition
Student: STU025 | Course: CRS02
=================================================================
Automated digitisation of hand-drawn diagrams from lecture whiteboards
improves accessibility for distance learning. This study fine-tunes
ResNet-50 and MobileNet architectures on a custom dataset of flowcharts and
UML diagrams.
Data augmentation techniques, including random affine transformations and
elastic distortions, mitigated overfitting on small training samples.
MobileNet achieved 92.4 percent top-1 classification accuracy with an
inference latency of 18ms per image.
We discuss future deployment as an edge service integrated into the
SmartEdU virtual learning environment for real-time lecture slide
synthesis.
