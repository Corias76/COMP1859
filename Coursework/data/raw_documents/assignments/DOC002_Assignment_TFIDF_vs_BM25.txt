Comparative Evaluation of TF-IDF versus BM25 on Course Corpora
Student: STU012 | Course: CRS01
=================================================================
In this coursework assignment, we empirically evaluate the performance of
TF-IDF against Okapi BM25 scoring functions. The benchmark dataset consists
of academic papers and lecture transcripts.
BM25 demonstrated superior ranking accuracy with Mean Average Precision
(MAP) of 0.82 compared to 0.74 for standard TF-IDF. This difference stems
from BM25 term frequency saturation and document length normalization
(parameters k1=1.5, b=0.75).
Furthermore, we analyzed query drift in pseudo-relevance feedback and
observed that Rocchio algorithm expansion improved recall by 14 percent for
short conversational queries.
