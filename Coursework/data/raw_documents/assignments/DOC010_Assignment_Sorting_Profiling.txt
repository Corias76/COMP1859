Empirical Profiling of Sorting Algorithms on Large Student Registries
Student: STU063 | Course: CRS05
=================================================================
This study provides empirical time and space profiling of Quicksort,
Mergesort, Heapsort, and Timsort when ranking academic cohorts by grade
point average and student IDs.
Python native Timsort exhibited exceptional stability and adaptive
performance on partially ordered datasets, completing in O(n) time on
pre-sorted departmental lists.
Memory consumption analysis highlighted Mergesort auxiliary buffer
overhead, while In-place Quicksort demonstrated optimal L1/L2 cache
locality on modern multi-core CPUs.
