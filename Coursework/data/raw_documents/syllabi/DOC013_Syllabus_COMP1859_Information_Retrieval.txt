Course Syllabus: COMP1859 Information Retrieval
Department: Computer Science | Course: CRS01
=================================================================
Course Overview: COMP1859 covers modern Information Retrieval systems,
algorithms, and practical applications. Students explore indexing, boolean
search, vector space models, and neural semantic retrieval.
Core Topics: Inverted index construction, tokenization, stemming, TF-IDF
weighting, Okapi BM25 ranking model, evaluation metrics (Precision, Recall,
MAP, NDCG), and structured vs unstructured data integration.
Assessment Criteria: Individual coursework prototype design and evaluation
(100 percent). Students develop an academic search system handling diverse
educational datasets.
