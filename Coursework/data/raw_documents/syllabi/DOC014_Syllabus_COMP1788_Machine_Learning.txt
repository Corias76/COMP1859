Course Syllabus: COMP1788 Machine Learning Systems
Department: Artificial Intelligence | Course: CRS02
=================================================================
Course Overview: COMP1788 introduces principles and design patterns for
engineering scalable machine learning systems in production environments.
Core Topics: Supervised and unsupervised learning, regression,
classification, deep neural networks, model deployment, monitoring data
drift, and pipeline automation.
Assessment Criteria: Coursework practical lab projects (50 percent) and
final system evaluation exam (50 percent). Emphasizes ethical AI and bias
mitigation.
