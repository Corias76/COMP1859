Course Syllabus: COMP1804 Algorithms & Data Structures
Department: Computer Science | Course: CRS05
=================================================================
Course Overview: COMP1804 focuses on algorithmic problem solving,
asymptotic complexity analysis, and fundamental abstract data types.
Core Topics: Big-O notation, recursion, divide-and-conquer, graph traversal
(BFS/DFS), shortest path algorithms (Dijkstra/A*), dynamic programming, and
greedy algorithms.
Assessment Criteria: Mid-term algorithmic coding challenge (40 percent) and
final examination (60 percent).
